# Antoine Khati, MD, MS

**PhD researcher | Applied machine learning · Longitudinal data science · Wearable sensing**

I'm a PhD researcher in Health Promotion Sciences at the **University of Connecticut** (expected graduation **May 2028**), with interdisciplinary training in medicine, health sciences, and engineering data science.

I study how physiological sensing, repeated assessments, and computational methods can improve our understanding of health and behavior. My research record includes **25+ peer-reviewed publications**, longitudinal statistical modeling, and collaborative work across clinical, behavioral, and technical teams.

## Research and engineering

- **Wearable sensing and ecological momentary assessment (EMA):** Integrated seven Garmin data streams with repeated self-reports, constructed temporally aligned exposure windows, and analyzed near-term behavioral outcomes using repeated-measures models, generalized estimating equations (GEE), and sensitivity analyses. Related manuscript **submitted to IEEE ICHI** (under review).
- **Research software:** Building **Ollie**, a private, in-development Python/FastAPI prototype for adaptive EMA workflows with persistent records and automated testing.
- **ML foundations:** Implemented vectorized logistic regression and a one-hidden-layer neural network in Python coursework, including forward propagation, backpropagation, cross-entropy loss, and gradient descent.
